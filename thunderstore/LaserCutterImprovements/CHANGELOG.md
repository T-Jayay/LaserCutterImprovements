# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release, for the Engineer's Laser Cutter.
- A range indicator while your beam reaches its maximum range without hitting anything.
- Your visible beam ends where the attack stops.
- `TurretHitboxScale`: a smaller hitbox for allied Engineer turrets, for your Laser Cutter only (off by default).
- `HealAndDamage`: heal mechanical allies without stopping the beam (off by default).
- Client-side: nobody else needs the mod.
- In-game settings with Risk of Options (optional). Needs HookGenPatcher (mod managers install it automatically).
